Privacy policy

LibreGeoTag · last updated 19 November 2026

LibreGeoTag turns a photograph into a location memory image. It is built to work without an account and without sending your photographs anywhere. This page describes exactly what the app does with your information — and, mostly, what it does not do.

In short

  • No account, no sign-in, no profile.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no tracking.
  • Your photographs never leave your device.
  • Location is read once, only when you ask for it, and never in the background.
  • The only network request the app can make is an address lookup from coordinates, and only when you tap that button.

What stays on your device

The photograph you choose is read from where it lives and is never modified, moved, or overwritten. A new image is written only when you tap Save or Share.

The location you attach, the address text, the note, and the date exist only in memory while the editor is open. The app keeps no history, no log, and no database of where you have been; closing the editor discards them.

Images you save go to your device's photo gallery, where they are visible to other apps that can read the gallery, exactly like any other photograph you save. Sharing hands the image to the app you choose.

What leaves your device

Address lookup

When you tap the button that looks up an address, the app sends the coordinates of the chosen location to Photon (photon.komoot.io), a geocoding service operated by komoot, which answers with a place name derived from OpenStreetMap data.

That request carries the coordinates and the information any HTTPS request carries: your IP address and the app's user-agent string. It carries nothing else — no photograph, no note, no device identifier, no account.

If you never tap the lookup, no request is made. The app works fully offline: you can choose a location and type an address by hand. How Photon handles that request is governed by its operator's privacy policy, not this one.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the finished image is passed to your device's share sheet and you choose the destination. Whichever app you pick then receives the image and handles it under its own terms. LibreGeoTag uploads nothing itself.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • It does not use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs, and does not read advertising identifiers.
  • It does not request background location, record a location history, or set up geofences.
  • It does not upload, back up, or sync your photographs.
  • It does not sell or share data with data brokers.

Permissions and why they exist

PermissionPurpose
Location (approximate and precise) To attach the place you are at, and only after you tap “Use my location”. Denying it leaves the app fully usable with a manually chosen location.
Storage (Android 9 and older only) To save the finished image to your gallery. Newer Android versions need no permission for this.
Internet To look up an address. Nothing else uses it.

The app has no camera or microphone permission. Taking a photograph opens your device's own camera app, and the resulting image is handled like any other photograph you pick.

Children

LibreGeoTag is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed. The project's git history keeps every earlier version.

Verifying this yourself

LibreGeoTag is free software under the MIT licence, and its source is public at github.com/wichosu/libregeotag. Every claim on this page can be checked against the code that makes it true.
