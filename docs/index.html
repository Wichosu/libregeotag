LibreGeoTag

Give your photographs a place and a story.

LibreGeoTag attaches a place, an optional note, and a date to a photograph and renders a new image — either with the text over the picture, or as a postcard with a margin and a caption. The original photograph is never modified.

Local-first: no account, no backend, no analytics, and your photographs never leave the device. Only coordinates are sent, and only to look up an address. Free software under the MIT licence, for Android.
